Delete Your Account & Data

Aurelia · Fabula Labs — Last updated: October 31, 2026

You can delete your Aurelia account and all associated data at any time.

Delete it in the app

Open Aurelia, go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm. Your account and the data listed below are deleted immediately.

Or request deletion by email

Email us from the email address associated with your Aurelia account, and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days:

hello@fabulalabs.net

Please use the subject line "Delete my Aurelia account" so we can process it quickly.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All saved aura readings (descriptions, traits, results)
  • All images you saved to your history
  • Photo fingerprints (short hashes kept so the same photo gets the same reading)
  • Your monthly usage counts

Delete individual readings yourself

Within the app, you can delete any individual saved reading (and its image) at any time from your history — without deleting your whole account.

Data retention

Deleting in the app takes effect immediately; an emailed request is processed within 30 days. Backups, if any, are purged on a rolling basis and are not used for any other purpose.

Feedback messages you sent from the app are kept without any link to your deleted account, so we can still act on a reported problem. Ask us at the address above and we will delete them too.
